Welcome to No. 9 Highland Grove a fantastic family home located in a quiet cul de sac in the heart of the well renowned The Park, Cabinteely. A wonderful opportunity to purchase a contemporary and updated three bedroomed semi-detached family home with the addition of a loft conversion.

Having been recently modernised by the current owners to include an extension to the hallway bringing in lots of natural light as well as triple glazed windows and a composite door. The additions of a guest W.C. downstairs and an attic room truly maximise the living space throughout this home.

The accommodation includes a welcoming entrance hall off which is a guest W.C. The living room with large box bay window, wooden mantle and electric stove has a south facing window maximising sunlight and brightness, French style double doors lead you to the open plan kitchen/dining room with direct access to the rear garden creating the perfect flow on the ground floor, ideal for entertaining. Upstairs there are 3 bedrooms (one of which boasts a fabulous view overlooking Dublin Bay to Howth Head) and a contemporary shower room. There is a bright wood panelled attic space with access to eaves storage with Velux roof light overlooking Dublin Bay.

This home is in an exceptional location ideally positioned on a quiet cul-de-sac with shops, cafes, schools, pre-schools and one of Dublins finest parks and playgrounds with a few minutes stroll. It is close to the centre of Cabinteely Village with its numerous gourmet restaurants, trendy cafes, bars and shops. The property is conveniently located just off the N11 with several bus routes and walking distance to the LUAS at nearby Carrickmines, providing frequent access to the city centre. The local library, numerous shopping facilities at Cornelscourt as well and Cabinteely House and park are also just a leisurely walk from the property. There are a wide range of excellent schools nearby including St Brigid's Girls and Boys NS, Holly Park, Loreto Foxrock and ease of access to many others.
